Weather Station - list of changes

1.3.0
  - Added the humidity sensor.
  - The report script prints daily minimum and maximum values.
  - Measurements are stored every 10 minutes instead of every 15 minutes.

1.2.0
  - Added air pressure readings.
  - Fixed the time zone of the timestamps (UTC is used everywhere).

1.1.0
  - Station name and location are read from config.json.
  - The logger retries reading a sensor three times before giving up.

1.0.0
  - First version: temperature readings only.
